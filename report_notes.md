Different powers have different strategies
- England: Focus on naval dominance and securing key coastal territories.
- France: Balance between land and sea, with a focus on alliances.
- Germany: Aggressive expansion in central Europe, aiming for quick territorial gains.
- Italy: Opportunistic play, often switching alliances to maximize gains.
- Austria-Hungary: Defensive play, focusing on maintaining control of the Balkans.
- Russia: Large-scale offensives, often coordinating with allies for multi-front attacks.
- Turkey: Defensive and strategic, often playing a long game to outlast opponents.

Basic Heuristics
- Always try to control supply centers, as they are crucial for building and maintaining armies and fleets.
- Always defend province if number of neighboring enemy units is greater than 1.
- Retreat if surrounded by enemy units and cannot support hold.
- Always attack province if number of neighboring friendly units is greater than 1.
- Prioritize moves that increase the number of controlled supply centers.
